---

**The Homework Helper Prompt**

Next time your child is stuck on an assignment, paste this into ChatGPT or Claude:

> *"Act as a patient, encouraging tutor for a [GRADE]-grader. My child is working on [SUBJECT/TOPIC] and is struggling with [SPECIFIC CONCEPT]. Do not give them the answer directly. Instead, break the concept down into simple steps using an analogy or real-world example they would find interesting (they like [CHILD'S INTEREST]). Ask them guiding questions to help them figure out the answer themselves. Keep your language warm, encouraging, and age-appropriate."*

**Why this works:**

Most parents either end up giving their kid the answer (because it is 8 p.m. and everyone is tired) or get frustrated trying to explain something in a way that clicks. This prompt turns AI into a patient tutor that:

- Connects the concept to something your child actually cares about
- Uses the Socratic method (guiding questions instead of direct answers)
- Keeps the tone encouraging so your child does not shut down

**Real parent feedback:**

> *"I used this prompt when my 9-year-old was melting down over fractions. I told the AI she loves baking, and it explained fractions using cookie recipes. She went from crying to actually excited. I almost cried too."*
> -- Sarah, mom of three

---

**Pro tip:** The more specific you are about your child's interests and where they are stuck, the better the AI response will be. Instead of "my child is struggling with math," try "my 3rd grader does not understand how to find equivalent fractions."

Specificity is the secret to great prompts. And that is exactly what we focus on in everything we create.
