# Privacy Policy

**Prompts for Parents**
**Website:** promptsforparents.org
**Last Updated:** March 2026

---

Your privacy is important to us. As a site built by parents, for parents, we understand the value of keeping your personal information safe. This Privacy Policy explains how Prompts for Parents ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects your information when you visit our website, subscribe to our newsletter, download prompt packs, or interact with our services.

By using our website, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of our site.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information You Provide Directly

- **Email address** when you subscribe to our newsletter or download free prompt packs
- **Name** (optional) when you subscribe or create an account
- **Contact information** when you reach out via email or contact forms
- **Payment information** when you purchase premium prompt packs or resources (processed securely through third-party payment providers; we never store your payment details)
- **Prompt feedback and suggestions** you submit through our site

### Information Collected Automatically

- **Device information:** browser type, operating system, screen resolution
- **Usage data:** pages visited, prompts viewed or downloaded, time on site, referring URLs
- **IP address** (anonymized where possible)
- **Cookies and similar technologies** (see Section 3 below)

### Information We Do NOT Collect

- We do not collect information about your children beyond general, non-identifying details you may voluntarily share in feedback or surveys (e.g., age ranges)
- We do not track what you type into AI tools using our prompts
- We have no access to your conversations with AI assistants

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Deliver prompt packs, newsletters, and resources you request
- Improve our website, prompt quality, and user experience
- Analyze site traffic and understand which prompts are most useful
- Send email communications you have opted into
- Display relevant advertising to support our free content
- Respond to your questions and feedback
- Comply with legal obligations

We will **never** sell your personal information to third parties.

## 3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies and similar technologies to enhance your experience.

### Essential Cookies
Required for the website to function properly, including remembering preferences and maintaining sessions.

### Analytics Cookies
**Google Analytics** -- We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our site, including which prompts are most popular and how users navigate our content. Data is collected in anonymized, aggregated form. You can opt out using the [Google Analytics Opt-Out Browser Add-on](https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout).

### Advertising Cookies
**Google AdSense** -- We display ads through Google AdSense to support our free content. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your browsing history. You can opt out of personalized advertising at [Google Ad Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/).

**Amazon Associates** -- Some of our content includes affiliate links to books, tools, and products on Amazon. Amazon may place cookies when you click these links. See [Amazon's Privacy Notice](https://www.amazon.com/privacy) for details.

### Email Marketing Cookies
Our email marketing platform may place cookies to track email engagement (open rates, click-through rates) to help us send more relevant content.

### Managing Cookies
You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some website functionality, such as remembering your preferences.

## 6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Prompts for Parents is a resource **for adult parents and caregivers**. Our website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

While our prompts are designed to help parents interact with their children, the website itself is intended for adult use only.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has submitted personal information to us, please contact us at privacy@promptsforparents.org. We will promptly delete such information.

We comply fully with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

## 9. Your Rights

### For All Users

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you
- **Correct** inaccurate personal data
- **Delete** your personal data (subject to legal requirements)
- **Unsubscribe** from emails at any time (one-click link in every email)
- **Opt out** of targeted advertising

### GDPR Rights (EEA Residents)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation, you have the right to:

- **Access** your data and receive a copy
- **Rectification** of inaccurate data
- **Erasure** ("right to be forgotten")
- **Restrict processing** of your data
- **Data portability** in a machine-readable format
- **Object** to processing for direct marketing or legitimate interests
- **Withdraw consent** at any time

**Legal basis for processing:** Consent (newsletter sign-up, downloads), contractual necessity (purchases), and legitimate interests (analytics and site improvement).

Contact privacy@promptsforparents.org to exercise your rights. We will respond within 30 days.

### CCPA Rights (California Residents)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, you have the right to:

- **Know** what personal information we collect and how it is used
- **Delete** your personal information
- **Opt out** of the sale of personal information (we do **not** sell personal information)
- **Non-discrimination** for exercising your rights

Contact privacy@promptsforparents.org with the subject line "CCPA Request" to exercise your rights.

## 11. AI Tools and Your Privacy

We want to be transparent about an important distinction:

- **Our website** collects the data described in this policy
- **AI tools** (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, etc.) that you use with our prompts are separate services with their own privacy policies
- We have **no access** to your AI conversations or any data you share with AI tools
- We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of any AI tool you use, especially regarding data retention and training

We design our prompts to minimize the personal information you need to share with AI tools. See our [Safety Commitment](/about#safety-commitment) for more details.
