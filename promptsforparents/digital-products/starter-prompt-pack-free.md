## Category 1: Homework Help

### Prompt 1 -- Explain a Concept at My Child's Level

> I need you to explain [topic, e.g., fractions / photosynthesis / the American Revolution] to my [age]-year-old child. Use simple language, real-world examples they would encounter in daily life, and check for understanding by asking 2-3 questions at the end. Avoid jargon. Make it fun and conversational.

**Customization tip:** Add your child's interests after the topic. For example, "My child loves soccer" lets the AI weave sports analogies into the explanation.

---

### Prompt 2 -- Step-by-Step Math Problem Solver

> My child is stuck on this math problem: [paste or describe the problem]. Please walk through the solution step by step, explaining the reasoning behind each step in language a [grade level] student can follow. Do not just give the answer -- teach the method so my child can solve similar problems independently.

**Customization tip:** Mention the specific math curriculum or textbook if you know it (e.g., "We use Singapore Math") so the AI matches the method your child is learning in school.

---

### Prompt 3 -- Book Report Helper

> My child needs to write a book report on [book title] by [author]. They are in [grade]. Help them organize their thoughts by asking them guided questions about the main characters, plot, setting, and theme. Then help them create an outline with an introduction, 3 body paragraphs, and a conclusion. Do not write the report for them -- guide them through the process.

**Customization tip:** Add the teacher's specific requirements (word count, format, due date) so the AI can tailor the outline to match.

---

### Prompt 4 -- Practice Quiz Generator

> Create a 10-question practice quiz for a [grade level] student studying [subject/topic]. Include a mix of multiple choice, true/false, and short answer questions. Make the difficulty level appropriate for [beginner/intermediate/advanced]. Provide an answer key at the end with brief explanations for each correct answer.

**Customization tip:** Ask the AI to focus on the specific chapters or concepts your child's upcoming test will cover.

---

### Prompt 5 -- Study Schedule Builder

> My child has the following upcoming tests and assignments: [list subjects, topics, and due dates]. They have [number] hours available for studying each day after school. Create a realistic study schedule that breaks the material into manageable chunks, includes short breaks, and prioritizes the most difficult subjects. My child is in [grade] and learns best by [visual/auditory/hands-on methods].

**Customization tip:** Include your child's extracurricular schedule (sports practice, music lessons) so the AI works around real commitments.

## Category 2: Meal Planning

### Prompt 6 -- Weekly Family Meal Plan

> Create a weekly meal plan (Monday through Sunday) for a family of [number]. We need breakfast, lunch, dinner, and one snack each day. Requirements: [list any dietary restrictions, e.g., nut-free, vegetarian, dairy-free]. Our grocery budget is approximately $[amount] per week. Prioritize meals that take 30 minutes or less on weeknights. Include a grocery list organized by store section.

**Customization tip:** Mention 3-5 meals your family already loves so the AI can build around familiar favorites and introduce variety gradually.

---

### Prompt 7 -- Use-What-We-Have Recipe Finder

> I have the following ingredients in my fridge and pantry: [list ingredients]. I need to make a [breakfast/lunch/dinner] that is kid-friendly and takes less than [time] to prepare. My children are ages [ages] and they are picky about [list dislikes]. Give me 3 recipe options with step-by-step instructions.

**Customization tip:** Include your cooking equipment limitations ("I only have a microwave and toaster oven") for more practical suggestions.

---

### Prompt 8 -- Lunchbox Ideas for the Week

> Give me 5 different school lunchbox ideas for my [age]-year-old. Requirements: no [allergens], no microwave available at school, food must stay fresh for [number] hours in a lunchbox with an ice pack. Include a main item, a fruit or vegetable, a protein-rich snack, and a small treat. Make them fun and appealing to kids. Keep prep time under 15 minutes each morning.

**Customization tip:** Add what your child ate last week so the AI avoids repeats and keeps things fresh.

---

### Prompt 9 -- Batch Cooking Plan for Busy Parents

> I have 2 hours on Sunday to batch cook for the week. I need to prepare components that can be mixed and matched into different meals for a family of [number]. Everyone in my family eats [dietary preferences]. Give me a detailed batch cooking plan with exact timing, quantities, and storage instructions. Show me how each prepped item turns into at least 2 different meals during the week.

**Customization tip:** Specify your kitchen equipment (slow cooker, Instant Pot, sheet pans) so the AI can optimize for parallel cooking.

---

### Prompt 10 -- Kid-Friendly Cooking Lesson

> I want to teach my [age]-year-old to cook. Suggest an age-appropriate recipe we can make together this weekend. It should involve [number] steps they can actively participate in, use safe techniques for their age, and result in something they will be excited to eat. Include safety reminders for each step and a list of everything we need before we start.

**Customization tip:** Mention what your child likes to eat and any prior kitchen experience so the AI picks the right difficulty level.

## Category 3: Bedtime Stories

### Prompt 11 -- Custom Bedtime Story with My Child as the Hero

> Write a bedtime story for my [age]-year-old named [child's name]. They love [interests, e.g., dinosaurs, space, mermaids]. The story should be about [theme, e.g., being brave, making friends, trying new things]. Make it calming and end with the character falling peacefully asleep. Keep it around [5/10/15] minutes of reading time. Use gentle, soothing language appropriate for bedtime.

**Customization tip:** Include your child's pet's name, best friend's name, or favorite toy to make the story feel personal and magical.

---

### Prompt 12 -- Continuing Adventure Series

> My child loves the bedtime story character [character name] we created last time. The character is a [brief description]. Write the next chapter of their adventure. In this episode, [character name] should [face a new challenge / visit a new place / meet a new friend]. Keep the tone consistent with a gentle bedtime story and end on a cozy note. This is episode [number] in the series.

**Customization tip:** Keep a short list of story details (character traits, sidekicks, places visited) and paste them in so the AI maintains continuity.

---

### Prompt 13 -- Story That Teaches a Lesson

> Write a short bedtime story for my [age]-year-old that gently teaches about [lesson, e.g., sharing, honesty, patience, handling anger, being kind to someone different]. The main character should be a [child/animal/fantasy creature] that my child can relate to. Do not make the lesson heavy-handed -- weave it naturally into an engaging story. End on a warm, positive note.

**Customization tip:** If your child is dealing with a specific situation (new sibling, starting school, a fight with a friend), mention it so the AI can mirror it in the story.

---

### Prompt 14 -- Choose-Your-Own-Adventure Bedtime Story

> Start a choose-your-own-adventure bedtime story for my [age]-year-old. Set it in [setting, e.g., an enchanted forest, a space station, an underwater kingdom]. At the end of each section, give 2 choices for what happens next. Keep each section short (about 1 minute of reading). Make all paths eventually lead to a peaceful, happy ending since this is a bedtime story. Start with the first section now.

**Customization tip:** Let your child pick the choices each night and paste their selection back to continue the story across multiple bedtimes.

---

### Prompt 15 -- Calm-Down Story for Restless Nights

> My [age]-year-old is having trouble settling down tonight. Write a very calming story that gradually slows in pace and includes guided relaxation. Start with a gentle adventure, then have the character find a cozy place to rest. Include descriptions of deep breathing, relaxing muscles, and feeling warm and safe. Use repetitive, rhythmic language. The story should take about 10 minutes to read aloud in a slow, soft voice.

**Customization tip:** Mention whether your child responds better to nature imagery (forests, oceans) or cozy indoor scenes (blanket forts, warm kitchens) for the setting.

## Category 4: Creative Activities

### Prompt 16 -- Rainy Day Activity Plan

> It is a rainy day and I need to keep my [age(s)]-year-old(s) entertained indoors for [number] hours. We have basic craft supplies (paper, markers, glue, scissors, tape) and common household items. Give me a schedule of 5 different activities with clear instructions for each. Mix creative, active, and educational activities. No screens. Include estimated time for each activity and any setup/cleanup notes.

**Customization tip:** List any extra supplies you have (playdough, watercolors, cardboard boxes) so the AI can get more creative with its suggestions.

---

### Prompt 17 -- Age-Appropriate Art Project

> Design a step-by-step art project for my [age]-year-old. We have: [list available supplies]. The project should take about [time] to complete, produce a result my child will be proud of, and involve skills appropriate for their age. Include a materials list, numbered instructions with tips for each step, and ideas for displaying the finished artwork.

**Customization tip:** Mention upcoming holidays, seasons, or themes (child's favorite animal, a book they just read) to make the project more meaningful.

---

### Prompt 18 -- DIY Science Experiment at Home

> Give me a safe, exciting science experiment I can do at home with my [age]-year-old using only common household items. Explain the experiment step by step, include what we will observe, and provide a simple kid-friendly explanation of the science behind it. Safety level: parent-supervised but child-hands-on. List exactly what we need before we start.

**Customization tip:** Mention what science topics your child is currently studying in school so the experiment reinforces classroom learning.

---

### Prompt 19 -- Birthday Party Activity Ideas

> I am planning a birthday party for my [age]-year-old. There will be [number] kids attending. The party theme is [theme]. The party is [indoor/outdoor] and lasts [number] hours. Give me a detailed party timeline with 4-5 activities or games that fit the theme, require minimal supplies, and keep all the kids engaged. Include a backup plan for each activity in case it does not work as expected.

**Customization tip:** Mention the age range of all attending kids so the AI adjusts activities to work for the youngest and oldest guests.

---

### Prompt 20 -- Creative Writing Prompts for Kids

> Give me 10 creative writing prompts for my [age]-year-old child. They enjoy [genres/topics they like]. Each prompt should spark their imagination and be open-ended enough for a short story, poem, or journal entry. Include 3 easy prompts, 4 medium prompts, and 3 challenging prompts. For each prompt, include a "stuck? try this" hint in case they need help getting started.

**Customization tip:** If your child resists writing, ask the AI to frame prompts as "tell me a story about..." that they can dictate while you type.

## Category 5: Parenting Advice

### Prompt 21 -- Handling a Specific Behavior Challenge

> My [age]-year-old is [describe the behavior, e.g., throwing tantrums at the store, refusing to share with siblings, lying about small things]. This has been happening for [time period]. I have tried [what you have already attempted]. Give me 3 different evidence-based strategies I can try, explain why each works from a child development perspective, and provide specific scripts for what to say in the moment. Keep the tone supportive and judgment-free.

**Customization tip:** Include your parenting style (gentle parenting, authoritative, etc.) so the strategies align with your values.

---

### Prompt 22 -- Age-Appropriate Chore System

> Help me create a chore system for my [age(s)]-year-old(s). I want to teach responsibility without it feeling like punishment. Suggest age-appropriate chores for each child, a fair reward/consequence system, and a visual chore chart I can make at home. Include tips for getting buy-in from reluctant kids and how to maintain consistency.

**Customization tip:** Mention whether you prefer allowance-based, privilege-based, or intrinsic motivation approaches so the system matches your family values.

---

### Prompt 23 -- Difficult Conversation Script

> I need to talk to my [age]-year-old about [difficult topic, e.g., a family member's illness, divorce, death of a pet, bullying, body changes]. Give me a script for how to start the conversation, age-appropriate language to use, questions to ask to check their understanding, and how to respond to likely follow-up questions. Also tell me signs that they may need to revisit the conversation later or talk to a professional.

**Customization tip:** Include your child's temperament (sensitive, anxious, very logical, emotionally expressive) so the AI adjusts the approach.

---

### Prompt 24 -- Screen Time Boundaries Plan

> Help me create a realistic screen time plan for my family. My kids are ages [ages]. Currently they get about [current amount] of screen time per day. I want to reduce it to [goal amount] without constant battles. Give me a step-by-step transition plan, alternative activities for each time slot screens currently fill, specific language to use when enforcing the new rules, and how to handle pushback. Be realistic -- I am a [working/stay-at-home] parent and sometimes need screens as a tool.

**Customization tip:** List the specific screens and apps your kids use most so the AI can suggest targeted alternatives for each one.

---

### Prompt 25 -- Weekly Family Meeting Agenda

> Help me plan a weekly family meeting for our family of [number] with kids ages [ages]. Create a template agenda that takes [15/20/30] minutes, includes everyone's input, covers the upcoming week's schedule, addresses any problems or conflicts, and ends on a positive note. Include specific discussion questions, a format that gives every family member a voice, and tips for keeping younger kids engaged throughout.

**Customization tip:** Mention recurring issues (scheduling conflicts, sibling arguments, homework struggles) so the AI builds those topics into the template.
