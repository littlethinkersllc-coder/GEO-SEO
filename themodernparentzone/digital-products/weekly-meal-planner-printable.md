### How to Use This Planner

1. **Plan your meals first.** Fill in the weekly meal grid below. Start with dinners, then work backwards to breakfasts and lunches. Check what you already have in the fridge and pantry before adding to the grocery list.
2. **Build your grocery list.** Transfer ingredients to the grocery list section, organized by store department so you can shop efficiently in a single trip.
3. **Prep on Sunday.** Use the Meal Prep Checklist to batch-cook grains, chop vegetables, marinate proteins, and portion snacks for the week ahead.
4. **Track your spending.** Log your grocery receipts in the Budget Tracker to stay on target and spot savings opportunities over time.
5. **Note dietary needs.** Use the Notes section to flag allergies, preferences, or nutrition goals so every family member is covered.

## Sunday Meal Prep Checklist

*Block 60-90 minutes on Sunday to set your family up for a smooth week. Check off each step as you go.*

### Grains & Starches (20 minutes)

- [ ] Cook a big batch of rice, quinoa, or pasta
- [ ] Bake potatoes or sweet potatoes for the week
- [ ] Prepare overnight oats for Monday-Wednesday breakfasts
- [ ] _______________________________________________
- [ ] _______________________________________________

### Proteins (25 minutes)

- [ ] Season and marinate meats for Monday-Wednesday dinners
- [ ] Hard-boil eggs (6-8) for breakfasts and snacks
- [ ] Cook ground meat or shredded chicken for multiple meals
- [ ] Portion and freeze any proteins for later in the week
- [ ] _______________________________________________

### Fruits & Vegetables (20 minutes)

- [ ] Wash and chop salad greens and store in damp paper towels
- [ ] Cut vegetables for snacking (carrots, celery, peppers, cucumbers)
- [ ] Dice onions, mince garlic, and store in containers
- [ ] Wash and portion fruit for lunchboxes
- [ ] _______________________________________________

### Snacks & Extras (15 minutes)

- [ ] Portion trail mix, crackers, or granola into snack bags
- [ ] Make a batch of energy bites or muffins
- [ ] Prepare smoothie packs (frozen fruit + greens in freezer bags)
- [ ] Fill water bottles and pack in fridge
- [ ] _______________________________________________

### Kitchen Reset

- [ ] Clean out fridge: toss expired items, wipe shelves
- [ ] Label all prepped containers with contents and date
- [ ] Set out slow cooker or Instant Pot for Monday dinner
- [ ] Load dishwasher and clear counters

**Money-Saving Tips from The Modern Parent Zone:**
- Buy produce that is in season -- it is cheaper and tastes better.
- Check your pantry and freezer before shopping to avoid duplicate purchases.
- Store-brand staples (flour, sugar, canned goods, spices) are almost always identical to name brands.
- Buy proteins in bulk when on sale and freeze in meal-sized portions.
- Plan at least one "pantry clean-out" meal per week using what you already have.
