# The Modern Parent Zone

## Summer Activity Planner

### 12 Weeks of Fun, Learning & Memories

**FREE Printable Download**

---

*Summer break is here -- 12 glorious weeks of no homework, no early alarms, and no packed lunches. But by Week 2, you will hear it: "I'm bored." This planner is your secret weapon. Print it out, hang it on the fridge, and hand your kids a marker. Every week is mapped. Every day has a purpose. And at the end of summer, you will have a journal full of memories instead of a blur of screen time. Enjoy it.*

---

### How to Use This Planner

1. **Print the full planner** and keep it in a binder or hang sections on the fridge.
2. **Sit down with your kids** at the start of summer and fill in the 12-week calendar together. Let them circle activities they are excited about.
3. **Set weekly goals** every Sunday evening using the goal-setting section.
4. **Check the supply list** at the start of each week so you have everything on hand.
5. **Fill in the memory journal prompts** together at the end of each week -- these become your family's summer scrapbook.
