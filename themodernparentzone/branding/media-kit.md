## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do you accept all partnerships?**
No. We only partner with brands whose products and values align with our audience. We decline partnerships that we do not believe serve our readers.

**Do you guarantee positive reviews?**
No. Our reviews are honest. If we encounter significant issues with a product, we will discuss them with you before publishing, but we will not publish a misleading review. This commitment to honesty is exactly what makes our recommendations valuable.

**How far in advance should we book?**
We recommend reaching out at least 4-6 weeks before your desired publish date, especially for sponsored articles and newsletter features. Display ads can often be accommodated on shorter timelines.

**Do you offer exclusivity?**
Category exclusivity is available for premium and long-term partners. Ask us for details.
