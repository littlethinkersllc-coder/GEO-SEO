### Project Overview

Designed and developed a comprehensive professional development course for university faculty at a large public university. The course focused on modern pedagogical practices, educational technology integration, and creating inclusive learning environments.

### Challenge

University faculty needed structured professional development to adopt new teaching methodologies, integrate technology effectively, and implement inclusive design practices in their courses. The existing approach lacked consistency and measurable outcomes.

### Solution

Created a multi-module professional development course using the ADDIE instructional design framework:

**Module 1: Foundations of Effective Teaching**
- Evidence-based pedagogical strategies
- Active learning techniques
- Student engagement methods

**Module 2: Technology Integration**
- LMS best practices
- Multimedia content creation
- Virtual and hybrid teaching tools

**Module 3: Inclusive Course Design**
- Universal Design for Learning (UDL) principles
- Accessibility compliance (Section 508)
- Culturally responsive teaching practices

**Module 4: Assessment and Continuous Improvement**
- Formative and summative assessment strategies
- Data-driven course improvement
- Student feedback integration

### Tools Used

- Articulate Storyline and Rise for eLearning modules
- Canvas LMS for course delivery
- Synthesia for video content
- Google Analytics for engagement tracking

### Design Approach

Applied SAM (Successive Approximation Model) for rapid prototyping and iterative design. Each module went through multiple review cycles with faculty stakeholders to ensure relevance and practical application.

### Results

- Faculty participants reported increased confidence in technology integration
- Measurable improvement in student satisfaction scores across participating departments
- Course materials adopted as standard onboarding for new faculty
