### Project Overview

Developed comprehensive curriculum spanning grades 7 through 12 at a large private boarding school. The project encompassed course design, instructional material development, and technology-integrated learning experiences for diverse student populations.

### Scope

- Grade levels: 7th through 12th grade
- Setting: Large private boarding school
- Approach: Standards-aligned, differentiated instruction
- Technology: Integrated educational technology tools and LMS

### Key Deliverables

- Complete course curricula for multiple subject areas across grades 7-12
- Differentiated instruction strategies for diverse learners
- Assessment frameworks aligned with learning objectives
- Technology integration plans for classroom and asynchronous learning
- Faculty training materials for curriculum implementation

### Design Approach

Applied backward design principles (Understanding by Design) to develop curriculum that starts with desired learning outcomes and works backward to instructional activities and assessments. Incorporated Universal Design for Learning (UDL) principles throughout.

### Results

- Successfully implemented across multiple grade levels
- Improved student engagement through technology integration
- Created sustainable, repeatable curriculum design templates
