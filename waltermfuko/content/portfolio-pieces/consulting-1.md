### Project Overview

Partnered with an enterprise client as an instructional design consultant to completely redesign their employee training program. The engagement covered needs analysis, curriculum development, eLearning module creation, and evaluation framework design.

### Challenge

The organization's existing training program relied heavily on static presentations and lacked engagement, consistency, and measurable outcomes. New employee onboarding took too long and retention of key concepts was low.

### Solution

Conducted a comprehensive needs analysis and redesigned the entire training program using a blended learning approach:

**Phase 1: Needs Analysis**
- Stakeholder interviews and job task analysis
- Learner persona development
- Gap analysis between current and desired performance

**Phase 2: Design and Development**
- Developed a blended learning curriculum combining self-paced eLearning, instructor-led sessions, and on-the-job practice
- Created interactive Storyline modules with scenario-based learning
- Built assessment instruments aligned with performance objectives

**Phase 3: Implementation**
- Deployed content through the organization's LMS
- Facilitated train-the-trainer sessions for internal team leads
- Provided implementation support and troubleshooting

**Phase 4: Evaluation**
- Applied Kirkpatrick's Four Levels of Evaluation
- Tracked completion rates, knowledge retention, and on-the-job performance
- Delivered ROI analysis to leadership

### Tools Used

- Articulate Storyline 360
- Rise 360
- Client's enterprise LMS
- Camtasia for video content

### Results

- Reduced onboarding time while improving knowledge retention
- Increased training completion rates
- Positive stakeholder feedback on learner engagement and content quality
