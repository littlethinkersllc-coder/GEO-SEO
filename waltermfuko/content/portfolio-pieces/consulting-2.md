### Project Overview

Engaged as an LMS consultant to lead the architecture, configuration, and deployment of an enterprise learning management system. The project included platform selection advisory, content migration, user role design, and analytics implementation.

### Challenge

The organization was transitioning from fragmented, manual training processes to a centralized digital learning platform. They needed expert guidance on LMS architecture that could scale across departments while maintaining ease of use for both administrators and learners.

### Solution

Led the full LMS implementation lifecycle as an external consultant:

**Discovery and Planning**
- Assessed organizational learning needs across departments
- Evaluated platform options against technical and pedagogical requirements
- Developed an implementation roadmap with phased rollout

**Architecture and Configuration**
- Designed user roles, permissions, and organizational hierarchies
- Configured course catalog structure and enrollment workflows
- Set up automated notifications and reporting dashboards

**Content Migration**
- Audited existing training materials for quality and relevance
- Migrated and reformatted content for the new platform
- Created templates for consistent future content development

**Training and Handoff**
- Developed administrator and instructor training guides
- Conducted live training sessions for internal teams
- Created a sustainability plan for ongoing platform management

### Tools Used

- Enterprise LMS platform (Canvas / Blackboard / Moodle, depending on client)
- SCORM and xAPI content packaging
- Google Analytics and LMS native analytics
- Python for data analysis and reporting

### Results

- Successful platform launch across multiple departments
- Streamlined training administration and reporting
- Internal teams fully trained to manage the platform independently
