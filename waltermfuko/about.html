Background

Designing for Impact

I'm an instructional designer with a background that spans higher education, K-12, and professional development. My work focuses on creating learning experiences that are accessible, engaging, and grounded in evidence-based design principles.

I hold a Doctor of Education (Ed.D.) with an Instructional Design concentration from South College, a Master of Business Administration (MBA) from Virginia Wesleyan University, and a Bachelor of Arts in Sociology and Business Management from Elms College.

My approach blends established instructional design frameworks like ADDIE, SAM, and Universal Design for Learning (UDL) with modern AI-augmented workflows. I use tools like Articulate Storyline, Rise, Canvas LMS, Synthesia, and Claude to create learning content that meets learners where they are.

I'm particularly passionate about accessibility. All of my work adheres to Section 508 compliance standards, and I design with UDL principles from the start rather than retrofitting for accessibility after the fact.

I use data tools including Python, Google Analytics, and Tableau to measure learning outcomes and continuously improve course effectiveness through the Kirkpatrick and Phillips ROI evaluation models.
