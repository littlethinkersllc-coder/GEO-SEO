Process & Tools

Instructional Design Process & AI Toolkit

Walter's methodology, tools, and modern AI-augmented workflow.

Design Philosophy

Every project begins with understanding the learner. I combine established instructional design frameworks with modern AI-augmented tools to create learning experiences that are accessible, engaging, and measurably effective.

My process is iterative and data-informed. I use rapid prototyping to test assumptions early, gather feedback often, and refine until the learning experience meets its objectives.

Primary Frameworks

ADDIE

The foundation of my systematic approach: Analyze, Design, Develop, Implement, Evaluate. I use ADDIE for large-scale curriculum projects where thorough front-end analysis and structured development phases produce the best outcomes.

SAM (Successive Approximation Model)

For projects that benefit from rapid iteration, I use SAM's agile-inspired approach. Quick prototyping cycles allow stakeholders to see working examples early and provide feedback that shapes the final product.

Universal Design for Learning (UDL)

UDL is not an add-on but a design foundation. I build multiple means of engagement, representation, and action/expression into every project from the start, ensuring accessibility is inherent rather than retrofitted.

AI-Augmented Workflow

I integrate AI tools strategically throughout the design process:

  • Claude for content structuring, learning objective refinement, and assessment design
  • Synthesia for scalable video-based instructional content
  • AI-assisted analytics for interpreting learner performance data and identifying improvement opportunities

Evaluation Approach

I measure impact using the Kirkpatrick model (reaction, learning, behavior, results) supplemented by Phillips ROI methodology when stakeholders need to quantify the business value of training investments.
