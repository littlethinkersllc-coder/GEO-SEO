K12 (7-12) Curriculum Design

Comprehensive curriculum development for a large private boarding school, grades 7 through 12.

Project Overview

This project involved designing and developing a comprehensive curriculum framework for a large private boarding school serving students in grades 7 through 12. The goal was to create an engaging, standards-aligned curriculum that supported diverse learners across multiple subject areas.

Challenge

The school needed a modernized curriculum that could accommodate a wide range of learning needs, from students requiring additional support to advanced learners seeking enrichment. The existing materials lacked consistency across grade levels and did not meet current accessibility standards.

Approach

Using the ADDIE framework and Universal Design for Learning (UDL) principles, I developed a systematic approach to curriculum redesign:

  • Conducted a comprehensive needs analysis across all grade levels and subject areas
  • Mapped learning objectives to state and national standards
  • Designed modular lesson plans with built-in differentiation strategies
  • Created assessment rubrics aligned to learning outcomes
  • Developed teacher guides with implementation support materials
  • Ensured all materials met Section 508 accessibility standards

Tools & Frameworks

  • ADDIE instructional design framework
  • Universal Design for Learning (UDL) guidelines
  • Canvas LMS for content delivery
  • Articulate Rise for interactive supplementary modules
  • Google Analytics for tracking student engagement metrics

Outcomes

The redesigned curriculum provided a consistent, accessible learning experience across all grade levels. Teachers reported improved student engagement and clearer alignment between instruction and assessment. The modular design allowed for ongoing iteration and improvement based on student performance data.
