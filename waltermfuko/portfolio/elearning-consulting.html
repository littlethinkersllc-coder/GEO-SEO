Consulting Project

AI-Augmented eLearning Course Development

Interactive eLearning modules built with modern authoring tools and AI-assisted content development.

Project Overview

This consulting engagement involved designing and developing a suite of interactive eLearning modules that leveraged AI tools alongside traditional authoring platforms. The project demonstrated how AI can accelerate the instructional design process while maintaining quality and accessibility standards.

Challenge

The client needed a large volume of high-quality eLearning content developed within a compressed timeline. Traditional development processes would not meet the deadline. The solution required finding ways to accelerate production without sacrificing instructional quality, learner engagement, or accessibility compliance.

Approach

  • Used Claude for rapid content structuring, learning objective refinement, and assessment item generation
  • Developed interactive scenarios and branching simulations in Articulate Storyline
  • Created microlearning modules in Articulate Rise for mobile-friendly delivery
  • Produced AI-generated video presentations using Synthesia for scalable video content
  • Applied UDL principles and Section 508 standards throughout all modules
  • Implemented a quality assurance process with subject matter expert review cycles

AI Integration Points

Content Development

Claude was used to draft initial content outlines, refine learning objectives using Bloom's taxonomy, and generate diverse assessment items. All AI-generated content went through subject matter expert review and instructional designer refinement before inclusion.

Video Production

Synthesia enabled rapid creation of presenter-style videos without the scheduling and production overhead of traditional video shoots. This was particularly valuable for content that needed frequent updates.

Quality Assurance

AI-assisted review helped identify accessibility issues, readability concerns, and alignment gaps between learning objectives and assessment items.

Tools & Technologies

  • Articulate Storyline 360 for interactive modules
  • Articulate Rise for responsive microlearning
  • Synthesia for AI-generated video content
  • Claude for content development assistance
  • Canvas LMS for delivery and tracking
  • SCORM packaging for cross-platform compatibility

Outcomes

The project was delivered ahead of schedule, with the AI-augmented workflow reducing content development time by approximately 40% compared to traditional methods. Learner engagement metrics showed strong completion rates and positive feedback on the interactive elements. The modular architecture allows the client to update and expand the course library independently.
