Higher Education

University Professional Development Course Design

Faculty development courses focused on pedagogy, accessibility, and instructional technology integration.

Project Overview

This project involved creating a series of professional development courses for university faculty. The courses aimed to strengthen teaching effectiveness by introducing evidence-based pedagogical strategies, accessibility best practices, and modern instructional technology tools.

Challenge

Many faculty members were subject matter experts in their disciplines but had limited formal training in instructional design or educational technology. The university needed a structured professional development pathway that was practical, time-efficient, and directly applicable to their teaching contexts.

Approach

I used the SAM (Successive Approximation Model) framework to iteratively design and refine the course series through rapid prototyping and faculty feedback:

  • Conducted a needs assessment through faculty surveys and department chair interviews
  • Designed a modular course series with self-paced and facilitated components
  • Created interactive scenario-based learning activities using Articulate Storyline
  • Built accessibility awareness modules that demonstrated real-world impact
  • Developed a micro-credentialing system to recognize course completion
  • Integrated LMS features for discussion, peer review, and resource sharing

Course Series Structure

Module 1: Foundations of Effective Teaching

Introduction to evidence-based teaching practices, active learning strategies, and backward design principles for course development.

Module 2: Accessibility in Higher Education

Comprehensive coverage of UDL principles, Section 508 compliance, creating accessible documents, and inclusive assessment design.

Module 3: Instructional Technology Integration

Hands-on exploration of LMS features, multimedia creation tools, video-based instruction, and AI-assisted content development.

Module 4: Assessment and Data-Driven Improvement

Strategies for formative and summative assessment, learning analytics interpretation, and using data to iterate on course design.

Tools & Frameworks

  • SAM (Successive Approximation Model) for iterative design
  • Universal Design for Learning (UDL) guidelines
  • Articulate Storyline and Rise for interactive content
  • Canvas LMS for course delivery and analytics
  • Synthesia for video-based instructional content
  • Kirkpatrick evaluation model for measuring training impact

Outcomes

The professional development series was adopted as a recommended pathway for all new faculty. Participants reported increased confidence in using instructional technology, a stronger understanding of accessibility requirements, and measurable improvements in student evaluation scores. The modular format allowed faculty to complete courses on their own schedule while still benefiting from peer interaction in facilitated sessions.
